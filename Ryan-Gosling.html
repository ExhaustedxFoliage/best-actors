Ryan Gosling

Ryan Thomas Gosling is a Canadian actor born on November 12, 1980, in London, Ontario. He began his career as a child star on Disney Channel’s The All New Mickey Mouse Club (1993–1995) before transitioning to film with roles in The Notebook (2004) and Half Nelson (2006). His other notable films include Drive, Blade Runner 2049, and The Big Short.

FAQ & Information

▶ What are Ryan Gosling's most famous Oscar nominations?

Ryan Gosling received Academy Award nominations for Best Actor for his roles in Half Nelson (2006) and La La Land (2016), and Best Supporting Actor for Barbie (2023).

▶ Does Ryan Gosling play any musical instruments?

Yes! He plays the piano and guitar. He famously learned to play jazz piano extensively for his role in La La Land without using a stunt double for close-up hand shots.
