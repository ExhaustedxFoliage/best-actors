IN THE SPOTLIGHT

Antony Starr

A master of complex characters.

Antony Starr is a New Zealand actor born on October 25, 1975, in Wellington, New Zealand. He is best known for playing Homelander in the television series The Boys. Before The Boys, Starr was known for playing Lucas Hood in the action series Banshee. He also played twin brothers Van and Jethro West in the New Zealand television series Outrageous Fortune.

His performances as complex and dangerous characters made him one of the most recognizable actors in modern television. His role as Homelander became especially popular around the world.

    How these numbers are counted

    Screen roles count credited acting entries in the source’s film and television tables, including voice work, narration, cameos, short films and television films. A series is not counted once per episode. Multiple characters within one entry count as one credit; separately listed character credits count separately. Duplicate entries for the same work are merged. Self appearances, archive footage, credit-only appearances, production-only work, music videos, advertisements, video games and unreleased projects are excluded.

    Feature films count released feature-length works in the film table, including credited narration in documentaries. Short films and television films are excluded. These are published-filmography totals, not a count of the four titles featured on this page.

    Leading film roles: 3 verified lead or co-lead performances — After the Waterfall, American Sausage Standoff and Cobweb. Television leads are excluded. This is a manual classification using film credits and the sources below.
