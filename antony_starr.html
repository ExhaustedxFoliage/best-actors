Antony Starr

Antony Starr is a New Zealand actor born on October 25, 1975, in Wellington, New Zealand. He is best known for playing Homelander in the television series The Boys.

Before The Boys, Starr was known for playing Lucas Hood in the action series Banshee. He also played twin brothers Van and Jethro West in the New Zealand television series Outrageous Fortune.

His performances as complex and dangerous characters made him one of the most recognizable actors in modern television.
