Ryan Thomas Gosling is a Canadian actor born on November 12, 1980, in London, Ontario.
He began his career as a child star on Disney Channel’s The All New Mickey Mouse Club (1993–1995) before transitioning to film with roles in The Notebook (2004) and Half Nelson (2006)

Gosling has received three Academy Award nominations for Half Nelson, La La Land (2016), and Barbie (2023), winning a Golden Globe for La La Land. His other notable films include Drive, Blade Runner 2049, and The Big Short. He has been in a long-term partnership with actress Eva Mendes since 2011, and they have two children together.
