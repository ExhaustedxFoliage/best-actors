Brad Pitt

William Bradley Pitt is an outstanding American actor and film producer born on December 18, 1963. He began his career in the late 1980s and quickly gained worldwide popularity thanks to striking roles in cult films such as Fight Club, Seven, and Interview with the Vampire. Over his long career, Pitt has received numerous prestigious awards, including two Academy Awards.
