Brad Pitt

Brief Biography

William Bradley Pitt is an outstanding American actor and film producer. He began his career in the late 1980s and quickly gained worldwide popularity thanks to striking roles in cult films such as "Fight Club", "Thelma & Louise", and "Interview with the Vampire".

Over his long career, Pitt has received numerous prestigious awards, including two Academy Awards (one as a producer for "12 Years a Slave", and the second as Best Supporting Actor for Quentin Tarantino's "Once Upon a Time in Hollywood"). Besides cinema, he is known for his active humanitarian work and passion for architecture.
