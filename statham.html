Jason Statham

Jason Statham is a famous British actor born on July 26, 1967, and a global action movie superstar. Before starting his acting career, he was seriously involved in sports and was a member of Britain's National Diving Squad for 12 years. His path in cinema began thanks to director Guy Ritchie, who cast him in Lock, Stock and Two Smoking Barrels and Snatch. Since then, he has starred in major action franchises including The Transporter, The Expendables, and Fast & Furious.
